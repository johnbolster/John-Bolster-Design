About

Independent Designer

I'm John Bolster, an independent designer with more than two decades of work behind me and a workflow built for what's next. I care about thoughtful design. Ideas that hold up over time, type set with care, and the small details people feel even when they don't notice them. AI is part of how I work, helping me research, iterate and push ideas further than I could alone. Identity, art direction, print or web, every project gets the same craft and curiosity.
